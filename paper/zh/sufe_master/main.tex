{\zihao{-4}\songti\sloppy
\setlength{\parindent}{0pt}
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{2em}
\hspace*{2em}金融文本分析同时包含确定信息问题与前瞻性断言。前者要求从截止时点以前的申报文件中读取或计算已陈述事实，后者要求判断尚未实现的未来结果。现有检索增强生成与断言验证通常预先固定输出接口，容易把前瞻性断言压成无保留的点答案，或把本可闭合的事实问题压成分类裁决。本文提出FinPlanRAG，将输出契约表述为路由变量。LLM路由器依据查询与截止时点元数据选择义务检索或AnchorEvolve。义务检索以类型化义务组织证据获取，并由LLM阅读器返回带证据编号的闭合答案。AnchorEvolve抽取稳定关系、行业模式和实例三层锚点，依据可靠性、作用域和时效更新有限结果空间上的信念，保留可追溯的证据状态；校准读出再将该状态转换为点答案、可信集或弃权。\par
\hspace*{2em}本文在DocFinQA 300题、227条XBRL核验收入指引断言、470条A股年报断言和150条FinDVer断言上，以DeepSeek Flash、GLM 5.3 Flash和Qwen3-8B评估框架。较大基座上的路由器恢复了接近oracle的混合效用，义务检索以更少证据取得更高准确率。模糊分支中，全动作读出相对受限门在三个基座分别降低0.749、0.581和0.696的决策损失，配对区间均排除零；FinPlanRAG在三个基座的准确率、平衡准确率、宏F1和决策损失上均为最高；在真实结果落在指引区间内的82条断言上，三个基座的融合损失均显著低于随机森林；去掉三层锚点结构后，三个基座的损失分别上升0.379、0.317和0.899。A股回测中，DeepSeek完整系统取得0.783的准确率和2.179的决策损失，高于季度年化的0.777和2.270。类型库扩展在DeepSeek上把测试损失从2.934降到2.907。\par
\hspace*{2em}本文的原创性体现在三个方面。第一，把混合查询的输出契约作为查询层路由变量，使闭合问答与前瞻性裁决共享时点约束而接口不同。第二，把证据资格判定作为先于信念更新的多类别阶段，并与按揭示日更新的类型可靠性耦合。第三，用全动作读出在同一个期望损失下比较点答案、裁决集和弃权。本文不主张全面优于所有基线；贡献在于可审计的证据资格判定协议和类型可靠性演化机制，提供现有选择性预测接口难以复制的结构粒度。\par

\vspace{0.6cm}
\markboth{Abstract}{Abstract}
{\arial\zihao{-4}\selectfont\sloppy
\setlength{\parindent}{0pt}
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{3em}
\hspace*{2em}Financial text analysis combines determinate questions, whose answers are stated or computable at a cutoff, with forward-looking claims, whose outcomes have not yet been realized. Existing retrieval-augmented generation and claim-verification systems usually fix the output interface in advance, which can reduce a forward-looking claim to an unqualified point answer or force a closed fact question into a classification verdict. We introduce FinPlanRAG, which treats the output contract as a routing variable. An LLM router selects obligation retrieval or AnchorEvolve from the query and cutoff metadata. Obligation retrieval organizes evidence acquisition around typed obligations and returns a closed answer with evidence identifiers. AnchorEvolve extracts stable-relation, industry-schema, and instance anchors, qualifies their reliability, scope, and timeliness, and updates a belief over a finite outcome space while preserving an auditable evidence state. A calibrated readout converts this state into a point answer, a credible set, or an abstention.\par
\hspace*{2em}We evaluate FinPlanRAG on 300 DocFinQA questions, 227 XBRL-verified revenue-guidance claims, 470 A-share annual-report claims, and 150 FinDVer claims with DeepSeek Flash, GLM 5.3 Flash, and Qwen3-8B. The routers built on the two larger backbones recover near-oracle mixed utility, and obligation retrieval improves accuracy with fewer evidence passages. On the vague branch, the full-action readout lowers decision loss by 0.749, 0.581, and 0.696 relative to a restricted gate, with all paired intervals excluding zero. FinPlanRAG has the highest accuracy, balanced accuracy, macro-F1, and lowest decision loss on every backbone. On the 82 claims whose realized outcome lies inside the guidance range, fusion significantly lowers loss relative to the forest on all three backbones. Removing the three-layer anchor structure raises loss by 0.379, 0.317, and 0.899. On the A-share backtest, the DeepSeek full system reaches 0.783 accuracy and 2.179 decision loss, above the run-rate baseline at 0.777 and 2.270. A validation-gated type-library extension lowers DeepSeek test loss from 2.934 to 2.907.\par
\hspace*{2em}The originality is threefold. First, the output contract of a mixed financial workload is formulated as a query-level routing variable, so closed-answer retrieval and forward-looking adjudication share the cutoff constraint while keeping different interfaces. Second, evidence qualification is an independent multi-type stage before belief update, coupled with type reliability that evolves on public reveal dates. Third, a full-action loss-aware readout compares point answers, non-singleton adjudication sets, and abstention under one expected loss. The paper does not claim uniform superiority over every baseline; it claims an auditable evidence-qualification protocol and type-reliability mechanism that provide a structural granularity not available at the same cost in existing selective-prediction interfaces.
