\setlength{\emergencystretch}{2em}
\hspace*{2em}金融文本分析同时包含确定信息问题与前瞻性断言。前者要求从截止时点以前的申报文件中读取或计算已陈述事实，后者要求判断尚未实现的未来结果。现有检索增强生成与断言验证通常预先固定输出接口，因而容易把前瞻性断言压缩为无保留的点答案，或把本可闭合的事实问题强制为分类裁决。本文提出FinPlanRAG，将输出契约表述为路由变量。LLM路由器依据查询与截止时点元数据选择义务检索或AnchorEvolve。义务检索以类型化义务组织证据获取，并由LLM阅读器返回带证据编号的闭合答案。AnchorEvolve抽取稳定关系、行业模式和实例三层锚点，依据可靠性、作用域和时效更新有限结果空间上的信念，保留可追溯的证据状态；校准读出再将该状态转换为点答案、可信集或弃权。\par
\hspace*{2em}本文在DocFinQA、227条XBRL核验收入指引断言和FinDVer上，使用DeepSeek Flash、GLM 5.3 Flash和Qwen3-8B，与六个具名基线进行统一评估。较大基座上的路由器恢复了接近oracle的混合效用，义务检索以较少证据取得更高准确率，AnchorEvolve的锚点后验在三套基座上均提供可测量的语义信号，包含LLM锚点信息的融合后验在GLM 5.3 Flash和Qwen3-8B上取得最高普通准确率；共享校准RF作为非LLM诊断，在决策损失上取得最低值。结果表明，契约路由负责选择推理接口，锚点演化负责组织前瞻性证据，校准读出负责控制最终风险。\par

\vspace{0.6cm}
\markboth{Abstract}{Abstract}
{\arial\zihao{-4}\selectfont\sloppy
\setlength{\parindent}{0pt}
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{3em}
\hspace*{2em}Financial text analysis combines determinate questions, whose answers are stated or computable at a cutoff, with forward-looking claims, whose outcomes have not yet been realized. Existing retrieval-augmented generation and claim-verification systems usually fix the output interface in advance, which can reduce a forward-looking claim to an unqualified point answer or force a closed fact question into a classification verdict. We introduce FinPlanRAG, which treats the output contract as a routing variable. An LLM router selects obligation retrieval or AnchorEvolve from the query and cutoff metadata. Obligation retrieval organizes evidence acquisition around typed obligations and returns a closed answer with evidence identifiers. AnchorEvolve extracts stable-relation, industry-schema, and instance anchors, qualifies their reliability, scope, and timeliness, and updates a belief over a finite outcome space while preserving an auditable evidence state. A calibrated readout converts this state into a point answer, a credible set, or an abstention.\par
\hspace*{2em}We evaluate FinPlanRAG on DocFinQA, 227 XBRL-verified revenue-guidance claims, and FinDVer with DeepSeek Flash, GLM 5.3 Flash, and Qwen3-8B against six named baselines. The routers built on the two larger backbones recover near-oracle mixed utility, obligation retrieval improves accuracy with fewer evidence passages, and the anchor posterior provides measurable semantic signal across all three backbones. The LLM-dependent fused posterior attains the highest standard accuracy on GLM 5.3 Flash and Qwen3-8B. A shared calibrated RF is reported separately as a non-LLM diagnostic and yields the lowest decision loss. These results establish a division of labor in which contract routing selects the inference interface, anchor evolution organizes forward-looking evidence, and calibrated readout controls output risk. FinPlanRAG therefore provides an auditable and calibratable design for financial text workloads that combine factual retrieval with future judgment.\par
}
