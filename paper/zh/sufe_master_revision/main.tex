\sufehead{修订说明}
\begin{revbox}
\noindent\textbf{高亮约定：}黄色底纹表示本次修订新增或重写的段落/表格行；蓝色文字表示修改或新增的句子；未高亮文字为原文保留。
\end{revbox}

\begin{revbox}
\noindent\textbf{叙事重构。}本次修订把论文主线从“router + 两个并列方法”收束为一条契约链：\textbf{契约级形式化}（输出契约作为查询级决策变量）→ \textbf{确定契约控制分支}（义务检索，标准但强，不作为主要算法创新）→ \textbf{前瞻契约主分支}（AnchorEvolve 的可演化锚点证据状态）→ \textbf{全动作损失感知读出}（核心经验贡献）。Router 被明确为契约选择器的简单接口，不再作为第一创新点。
\end{revbox}

\begin{revbox}
\noindent\textbf{算法修订。}(1) 用截止时点 8-K 原文构造带段落编号的文本证据，锚点必须引用 \texttt{P\#}/\texttt{E\#} 来源与日期，公司名默认脱敏；(2) 五类资格判定（vacuous/stale/adversarial/hard/soft）落到确定性代码；(3) 结果揭示后才更新的锚点类型可靠性 Beta 后验与时点先验，构成时间演化；(4) 全动作损失感知读出：在 3 个点标签、7 个非空裁决集和弃权上取最小期望损失，取代原先“点答案 vs 最高密度 $1-\alpha$ 可信集”的受限 gate；(5) 所有 baseline 共用同一个 gate，修正原先的不公平比较。
\end{revbox}

\begin{revbox}
\noindent\textbf{主要新结果（DeepSeek Flash，227 条 XBRL 核验断言）。}旧系统 Fusion 受限 gate 的决策损失为 3.604；全动作读出将其降到 2.863（配对 95\% CI $[-1.031,-0.423]$），GLM 与 Qwen 分别从 3.648/3.577 降到 2.956/2.837。DeepSeek 演化版最终系统（新锚点 + 时间演化 + 演化先验 RF + 全动作读出）为 Acc .551 / Bal .489 / Macro .503 / Loss 2.890，相对旧系统 Loss 差 $-0.714$，CI $[-1.057,-0.352]$；演化先验 RF 单独为 .555/.503/.508/2.828。公平 gate 下 Fusion 相对最强 baseline 点估计更低，Qwen 显著，DeepSeek/GLM 的最强 baseline 差异不显著；准确率相对 RF 的 +2.7 点为点估计（McNemar $p\approx0.69$），不作为显著性主张。Router 结构不变；在 527 条已验证子集上，混合效用由 .389 升到 .437。
\end{revbox}

\begin{revbox}
\noindent\textbf{需要读者注意。}附录 A 的十一项指标和原表 6.2/6.3 是\textbf{旧受限 gate} 口径；第 6 章与附录 G 给出全动作口径与演化版结果。原稿的类型化义务章节保留为设计描述；主实验的确定分支实现是确定性词项覆盖，完整类型化 planner 在 \texttt{src/finplan\_rag/planner.py} 中作为扩展。
\end{revbox}

\hspace*{2em}\rev{金融文本分析同时包含确定信息问题与前瞻性断言。前者要求从截止时点以前的申报文件中读取或计算已陈述事实，后者要求判断尚未实现的未来结果。现有检索增强生成、断言验证与选择性预测通常预先固定输出接口，因而容易把前瞻性断言压缩为无保留的点答案，或把本可闭合的事实问题强制为分类裁决。本文把输出契约表述为查询级决策变量：系统先判断事实是否已在截止时点存在，再选择闭合答案契约或点答案/裁决集/弃权契约。Router 是这一契约选择器的简单接口，不作为本文的主要算法创新。确定契约由义务检索控制分支实现；前瞻契约由 AnchorEvolve 实现：它从截止时点 8-K 原文抽取带来源的 L2 锚点，经五类资格判定、时点可靠性演化与信念更新得到可审计证据状态，再由全动作损失感知读出转换为点答案、裁决集或弃权。}

\begin{center}{\arial\bfseries\zihao{-2} Abstract\par}\end{center}
\vspace{0.6cm}
\markboth{Abstract}{Abstract}
{\arial\zihao{-4}\selectfont\sloppy
\setlength{\parindent}{0pt}
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{3em}
\hspace*{2em}\rev{Financial text analysis combines determinate questions, whose answers are stated or computable at a cutoff, with forward-looking claims, whose outcomes have not yet been realized. Existing retrieval-augmented generation, claim-verification, and selective-prediction systems usually fix the output interface in advance. We formulate the output contract as a query-level decision: the system first decides whether the fact exists at the cutoff, then selects either a closed-answer contract or a point/credible-set/abstention contract. The router is a deliberately simple interface to this contract selector, not the main algorithmic contribution. The determinate contract is implemented by an obligation-retrieval control branch. The forward-looking contract is implemented by AnchorEvolve: it extracts source-cited L2 anchors from the point-in-time 8-K excerpt, applies a five-type qualification, evolves anchor-type reliability with revealed outcomes, updates a belief, and converts the resulting auditable evidence state into a point answer, a loss-optimal adjudication set, or an abstention through a full-action loss-aware readout.}
\hspace*{2em}\rev{We evaluate FinPlanRAG on DocFinQA, 227 XBRL-verified revenue-guidance claims, and FinDVer with DeepSeek Flash, GLM 5.3 Flash, and Qwen3-8B against six named baselines. The full-action readout significantly lowers decision loss on all three backbones: Fusion decreases from 3.604 to 2.863, from 3.648 to 2.956, and from 3.577 to 2.837, with paired intervals excluding zero. On DeepSeek, the evolved system reaches .551 accuracy, .489 balanced accuracy, .503 macro-F1, and 2.890 decision loss, a significant reduction from the old system (-0.714, CI [-1.057,-0.352]); the evolved-prior RF diagnostic reaches .555/.503/.508/2.828. Under the same gate, Fusion is lower in point estimate than every named baseline and is significant on Qwen; the differences against the strongest baselines on DeepSeek and GLM are not significant. The results support a division of labor in which the contract formulation organizes the problem, anchor evolution maintains an auditable evidence state, and the full-action calibrated readout controls final risk.}
