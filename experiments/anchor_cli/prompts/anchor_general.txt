Evidence retrieved from the open web. Each item has an ID, title, publication date, URL, and an excerpt. A date of "unknown" means the publication date could not be verified.
$evidence

Task:
1. Extract explicit L2 anchors that bear on the claim. An anchor is a fact, constraint, forecast, or disclosure practice stated in the evidence, not a restatement of the claim.
2. For every anchor, assign the outcome label it implies, a calibrated reliability, and a verbatim quote from the cited evidence item.
3. For every numeric anchor, fill numeric_value, numeric_unit, numeric_period and model_role. The model_role must be one of the roles listed above; use null when the number does not fill a model slot. The engine will perform the arithmetic, not you.
4. Score the outcome labels. The scores are marginal probabilities over the labels and must sum to 1.
5. If the claim is a disclosure-date claim, also give a point date and an interval for the expected disclosure date.

Rules:
- Each anchor must cite one W# source_id from the evidence list. An anchor without a valid source is rejected.
- The quote must be copied verbatim from the cited item, in the original language. Do not paraphrase inside the quote field.
- The anchor date must be no later than the cutoff. If the evidence date is unknown, set date to null and reliability no higher than 0.55; such an anchor cannot be hard.
- reliability is your calibrated probability that implied_label is correct, not your confidence in the reasoning. Use 0.5 when the evidence is weak.
- hard=true only when an explicit, verified constraint rules out at least one label (for example, cumulative revenue for the first three quarters already exceeds the full-year threshold). Most management commentary and analyst forecasts are soft.
- Evidence from an exchange announcement record (provider cninfo) is stronger for a disclosure date than a media report. Treat the announcement date as the recorded disclosure date and cite it as a disclosure_history anchor.
- A regulatory deadline is an upper bound, not evidence that the company will disclose late. Only assign the late label when the evidence states that the company expects or has scheduled a late disclosure.
- A date mentioned inside a quote must not be later than the cited source's publication date. If the source was published before an event, do not present that event as known from the source.
- For a disclosure claim, do not infer "未发布指引" from the absence of a guidance in a quarterly or annual report. Use the negative label only when the evidence explicitly states that no guidance was issued. A quarterly result is not evidence of absence.
- Do not invent numbers. If the evidence does not support a numerical anchor, leave the value as a short qualitative statement.
- numeric_value must be a plain number in numeric_unit, not a string with words. The number must appear in the quote. If the quote gives a range, put the midpoint in numeric_value and the bounds in numeric_low/numeric_high; do not silently pick a bound.
- Keep the unit exactly as written in the quote. If the quote says 500 亿元, set numeric_value=500 and numeric_unit=亿元. Do not convert it to 50,000,000,000 元; the engine needs the source unit.
- For a level or growth claim, extract the latest cumulative actual (for example 2026Q1-Q3) as ytd_actual before extracting any target. A target alone is not a forecast and cannot fill the ytd_actual slot.
- numeric_period is required for every numeric anchor. Use the period of the number, for example 2026Q1-Q3, 2026H1, 2026Q3, 2026Q2 or 2025FY. Do not leave it empty.
- Use model_role only when the number is the actual value for that slot. For example, a 2026 incentive target is fy_target, not ytd_actual; a Q3 single-quarter result is q3_revenue, not fy_actual. If a number is a forecast by an analyst rather than the company, use a role such as consensus or analyst_forecast, never ytd_actual.
- current-year slots (ytd_actual, q3_revenue, current_margin, current_capacity, capacity, utilization, yield) must use the latest target-year period. A 2025 number belongs in prior_actual or prior_margin, not in a current-year slot.
- A company target or an analyst consensus is not a point forecast for the model bridge. Put it in fy_target or consensus; the engine will not use it as ytd_actual or q4_forecast. If the evidence does not give a Q4 forecast, q3_revenue plus seasonal_factor is the preferred bridge input; without either, the engine reports a missing input or a run-rate baseline.
- If the evidence is insufficient, say so in missing and keep the label scores close to the prior.
- label_scores must use exactly the labels listed above and must sum to 1.
- For a metric claim, date_estimate must be null. If the evidence supports a numeric estimate of the metric, also return numeric_estimate with a point value, a low/high interval, the unit, the basis, and the W# sources used. The numeric estimate is auxiliary and must not change the label scores or the action. Do not invent a number when the evidence only supports a direction.
- For a date claim, numeric_estimate must be null, and date_estimate must contain point, low, high and basis. The point must lie inside the interval. The interval is a forecast, so it may be after the cutoff.
